About

Building things that think.

I'm currently pursuing my Master's in Computer Science, with research focused on autonomous intelligent agents, large language model applications, and scalable data pipelines. My work sits at the boundary where theoretical machine learning meets real-world systems.

Before grad school, I spent time building ML-powered products, experimenting with reinforcement learning environments, and contributing to open-source data tools. I believe the most interesting problems emerge when you treat AI not as a feature, but as architecture.

When I'm not reading papers or writing code, you'll find me exploring new datasets, tinkering with side projects, or thinking about how intelligent systems can be made more reliable and interpretable.
